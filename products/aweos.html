About the project

AWEOS is a Linux-kernel-based operating system project designed to combine a familiar desktop with advanced control. Its direction includes a Wayland desktop, AWEStore, accessible installation, first-boot setup and terminal tools. AWE-specific work is kept outside the protected /linux source tree.
